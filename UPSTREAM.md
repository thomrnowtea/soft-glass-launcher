# Upstream

Soft Glass Launcher is based on [Gathin23/cleanyourtv](https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv), revision `687b5ee2a3f3151f5d5e09d82b693d60fa626bdb` (v2.4).

This project adds a glass interface, Manrope typography, a six-app viewport with app ordering, first-run setup and optional Open-Meteo, WiZ, Stremio and Spotify integrations. Inputs, app discovery and system actions retain and extend the native Android foundation.

The original MIT copyright and license are retained in LICENSE and in APK assets. Manrope and Outfit font licenses are included under licenses/ and app assets. No device-audit history, private account data or publisher signing keys are part of this repository.
