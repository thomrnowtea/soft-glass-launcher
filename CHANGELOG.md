# Changelog

## 4.0.0

Initial public distribution of Soft Glass Launcher.

- Resumable first-run setup with optional integrations and neutral defaults.
- Glass materials, Manrope, light/dark/system themes and reduced motion.
- Six visible apps, scrolling rail, app ordering and contextual app actions.
- Weather by selected city, WiZ on/off controls, Stremio Continue Watching and Spotify playback indicator.
- Android input/settings discovery and optional startup guide.
- Independent `com.softglass.launcher` identity and release signing certificate.

Other TV brands, firmware and integration combinations require user testing.
