# Interactive prototypes

Open [launcher/index.html](launcher/index.html) or [onboarding/index.html](onboarding/index.html) in a browser. Arrow keys and Enter simulate remote navigation. Theme/language controls sit outside the product viewport.

These prototypes use demo apps, media, weather and connection state. They do not sign in to accounts or control a TV or lights. The Android APK reads the installed-app catalogue and opens real setup screens. Manrope is bundled locally under its OFL license; the launcher artwork demo may fetch public remote images.

The Tenzing backdrop is demonstration film artwork sourced from TMDB. Film artwork and app trademarks belong to their respective owners and are not covered by the code MIT license.
